# Product reference (concise)

## What Hui is

A privacy-first PWA for **recurring groups and gatherings**—families, friends, and similar circles who meet on a rhythm and need fair coordination without a group chat app.

## Core promise

> The app proposes, coordinates and remembers. The group decides. Nothing becomes final until the required participants agree.

## Primary use cases

- Recurring gatherings with configurable cadence
- Consensus-first scheduling (proposals, responses, deadlines)
- Host rotation
- Contribution coordination and fairness
- Dietary information
- Event memories and history

## Secondary capability

- Lightweight **one-off events** for occasional groups (same event machinery as recurring)
- Members may propose events when the group allows it
- Optional **reconnect** reminders after configurable inactivity

## Out of scope for MVP

- In-app chat
- Calendar import or live calendar sync
- Google/Microsoft calendar connection, free/busy, clash detection

## In scope later (MVP direction)

- **Calendar export only** for confirmed events

## Configurable per group (defaults TBD in implementation)

- Maybe / “can make work” responses (can be disabled)
- Minimum acceptable attendees
- Proposal deadlines
- Admin veto and host veto
- Who may propose events
- Admins manage membership

## Audience and commercial posture

Initial use is private among friends and family; running costs should stay low. Avoid choices that block a future commercial offering, but do not over-build for scale in Phase 0–1.
